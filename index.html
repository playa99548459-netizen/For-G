A letter from me to you

My G, ♡

I don't even know how to fit everything I feel for you into a few words, but I want to try.

Sometimes I really wish I could just be there with you. Not even to do anything big—just sit beside you, hold your hand, look at your face when you smile, annoy you a little, and hug you whenever you need one. I miss the little moments we can't have yet.

Long distance can be hard. There are times when I wish a screen wasn't the only thing between us, when I wish I could turn a “goodnight” text into a real goodnight kiss and a “I miss you” into the tightest hug. But even with all this distance, you still make my days feel brighter just by being you.

I hope you never think that being far away means you're any less important to me. You're in my thoughts in the randomest moments—when I hear a song, when something funny happens and I want to tell you, or when I imagine how nice it would be if you were right here.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise that I'll keep trying to understand you, care for you, make you smile, and remind you that you're loved. I want us to collect real memories one day: little dates, silly pictures, long hugs, and all the ordinary things that will feel special because they're with you.

If I could teleport anywhere right now, G, I'd choose wherever you are. Until I can, please imagine me holding your hand through this letter and giving you the biggest hug.

Thank you for being you, for being part of my life, and for giving me someone so special to miss. I miss you more than these words can explain, and I'm looking forward to the day distance is just a story we tell.

All my love, always. ♡
